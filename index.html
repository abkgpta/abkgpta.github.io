How to read it
  • Distance = how strongly it's linked to the sun in general
  • Size = how relevant it is to your line of reasoning
  • Violet = cause: streams flow in to the centre
  • Cyan = effect: streams flow out from the centre
  • More frequent streams = more relevant here
  • No colour = related, neither cause nor effect
  • Glow = hidden lead: loosely linked, but big here
  • Ring = can be unfolded in this sketch
