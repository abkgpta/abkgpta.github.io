Connection

Running on your Claude plan, no key needed. The first search asks your permission. Research here comes from Claude’s own knowledge (no web browsing), so sources are named but not linked.

No key? Open Celestial on claude.ai to research on your own Claude plan instead. That only works on claude.ai, not on this site.

How to use

  1. Ask a question in the search bar, like “Why is my bread dense?”, or type a topic to learn about.
  2. Click an orb to open it and read what it is, what to check and where the information comes from.
  3. Mark it ✓ relevant or ✕ not relevant. The map rearranges around your answers: related orbs move closer, ruled-out ones fade away.
  4. Read the running answer in the panel that appears bottom left after your first tick. It updates every time you mark an orb.
  5. Expand an orb to make it the new centre and dig a level deeper. The path at the top takes you back.

Where and how big

  • Closer to the centre: more strongly linked to it
  • Bigger: more relevant to your question
  • Glow: a hidden lead, loosely linked but worth checking
  • Double outline: can be expanded straight away

Colours

  • Violet: a cause of the centre
  • Cyan: an effect of the centre
  • Grey: related, neither cause nor effect
  • Green, thick: you marked it relevant
  • Faded, dashed: you marked it not relevant

Lines and streams

  • Streams flow into the centre from causes and out to effects. Longer and thicker means a stronger link
  • Faint line to the centre: a cause or effect link
  • Faint line between orbs: they are strongly linked to each other
