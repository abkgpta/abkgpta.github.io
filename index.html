How to read it
  • Distance = how strongly it's linked to the centre in general
  • Size = how relevant it is to your line of reasoning
  • Violet outline = cause: streams flow in to the centre
  • Cyan outline = effect: streams flow out from the centre
  • Grey outline = related, neither cause nor effect
  • Longer, thicker streams = stronger link. More frequent = more relevant
  • Glow = hidden lead: loosely linked, but big here
  • Double outline = can be unfolded in this sketch
